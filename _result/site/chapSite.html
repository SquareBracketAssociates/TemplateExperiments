6 Publishing a book as a web site

Pillar builds this book as a web site with one page per chapter. The page you read now was built that way.

Listing 6.1 writes the site in _result/site: open _result/site/index.html in a browser, or copy the folder to any web server. The site needs no server-side program.

6.1 The pages

  • index.html holds the text before the first chapter, usually the introduction.
  • Each chapter (each ## header) becomes its own page, named after the anchor of its header, (chapSyntax.html for Chapter 1), or after its title when it has no anchor (extensions.html).
  • Every page shows the table of contents with the chapters and their sections, and links to the previous and next chapters.

6.2 Configuration

The book is configured in pillar.conf:

  • htmlWriter must be michtml, the writer that numbers headers, figures and listings and links references across pages. The writer named micbasichtml writes plain HTML, without numbers.
  • numbering is true by default; set it to false to remove all numbers.
  • title is the title of the home page, and attribution appears in the footer.
  • palette chooses the colors: sky (light blue with orange highlights, the default), sunset (orange with blue highlights) or classic.
  • theme forces light or dark. Without it, the site follows the light or dark mode of the reader's system.

6.3 Style

Two files of _support give the site its look; change them to change it.

  • _support/templates/html/site.mustache is the template of every page: the table of contents, the pager and the footer. It receives the values content, title, bookTitle, toc, previous and next, and the settings of pillar.conf.
  • _support/html/css/book.css is the style sheet. It uses local fonts only, has a light and a dark variant of each palette, and has a print layout without navigation.

6.4 What the site does not show yet

  • Mathematics (Section 3.2) and footnotes (Section 4.1).
  • Anchors on tables.
